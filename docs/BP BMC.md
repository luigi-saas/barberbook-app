### **Business Plan**

### **1. Executive Summary:**

- **Product Name**: **BarberBook.ma**
- **Product Type**: SAAS (Software as a Service) platform.
- **Target Market**: Moroccan barbershops (initially focusing on major cities like Casablanca, Rabat, and Marrakech), followed by expansion to other parts of North Africa.
- **Problem Solved**:
    - Barbershops struggle with manual scheduling, managing appointments, customer relationships, and pricing transparency.
    - Customers often face long wait times, and there's a lack of streamlined booking systems for barbers.
- **Solution**: **BarberBook.ma** offers a digital platform where barbershops can manage profiles, barbers, services, and bookings, while customers can easily book appointments online, check services, and read reviews.
- **Revenue Model**: Monthly/annual subscription fees for barbershops, transaction fees on bookings, and premium service packages for additional features.

### **2. Market Research and Analysis**:

- **Market Size**: The Moroccan grooming market, which includes barbershops, salons, and spas, is rapidly growing. In cities with a high population of young, tech-savvy consumers, there is an increasing demand for convenience-driven services like online booking and appointment management.
- **Competition**: Some competitors may include local booking systems, but **BarberBook.ma** will differentiate itself by focusing on barber-specific features, such as barber profiles, skills, and personalized service management.
- **Target Audience**:
    - **Primary**: Barbershop owners looking for easier management of operations.
    - **Secondary**: Customers who value convenience and personalized grooming experiences.

### **3. Marketing Strategy Resp: Hasnae EL AMRANI** ❤️‍🔥:

- **Digital Marketing**: Instagram, TikTok, Facebook ads to target younger, tech-savvy customers.
- **Partnerships**: Collaborate with local barber schools, grooming influencers, and well-known barbershops.
- **Referral Program**: Incentivize barbershops to bring on more users (both barbers and customers) with referral bonuses.
- **Community Building**: Build an online community where barbers can exchange tips, share their work, and engage with customers. This would help drive long-term engagement and brand loyalty.

### **4. Monetization Strategy**:

This is where we really focus on making **BarberBook.ma** rentable and scalable.

1. **Subscription Plans for Barbershops**:
    - **Free Plan**: Basic features with limited access (e.g., one barber, limited bookings per month).
    - **Standard Plan**: Monthly subscription (e.g., MAD 200-300/month) with the ability to manage multiple barbers, unlimited bookings, and basic service customization.
    - **Premium Plan**: Higher monthly fee (e.g., MAD 500/month) with advanced features like analytics, customer segmentation, and premium placement in the app’s search results.
    - **Enterprise Plan**: Custom pricing for large chains with additional support, integrations, and custom features.
2. **Transaction Fees**:
    - **Booking Fee**: Charge a small percentage on every booking made through the platform. This could be around **2-5%** of the total transaction.
    - **Premium Listing Fees**: Barbershops can pay a fee to appear at the top of search results or be featured in promotional campaigns.
3. **Add-On Services**:
    - **Promotions and Discounts**: Offer barbershops the ability to run paid ads, promote special offers, or discount packages to attract more customers.
    - **Customer Management Tools**: Offer premium CRM features for barbershops to manage customer loyalty programs, track repeat customers, and personalize offers.
4. **Advertising**:
    - **Brand Partnerships**: Work with grooming brands, equipment manufacturers, or local businesses to display ads within the app. Barbershops could also advertise their services on the platform.

### **5. Financial Projections**:

- **Year 1**: Focus on acquisition of early customers, gathering feedback, and growing the user base.
    - **Revenue from Subscriptions**: Estimate based on a small number of subscribers (50-100 barbershops).
    - **Revenue from Transaction Fees**: Estimate based on average booking amounts and expected volume.
- **Year 2-3**: Focus on scaling up by adding more cities, features, and partners.
    - **Revenue Growth**: Predict significant growth in both subscription plans and booking transactions.

### **Business Model Canvas**

The **Business Model Canvas** helps map out the key components of the business. Here’s a breakdown for **BarberBook.ma**:

### **1. Key Partners**

- **Payment Processors** (Stripe, PayPal, local payment systems): To handle online payments securely.
- **Barbershops and Barbers**: Key users who provide the core service.
- **Tech Developers**: For continuous platform development and maintenance.
- **Marketing Agencies/Influencers**: To help with promotions and brand visibility.
- **Local Grooming Product Brands**: Potential for advertising and partnerships.
- **Salon Software Integration**: Potential partnerships for integration with existing tools.

### **2. Key Activities**

- **Platform Development**: Continual development of the website/app for usability and functionality.
- **Marketing & Customer Acquisition**: Running campaigns to onboard barbershops and customers.
- **Customer Support**: Offering support for barbershops and clients using the platform.
- **Data Analytics**: Gathering insights from platform usage to improve features and customer experience.

### **3. Key Resources**

- **Technology Infrastructure**: Servers, APIs, software development tools.
- **Customer Support Team**: Handling user queries and issues.
- **Sales & Marketing Team**: To help grow the customer base.
- **Branding**: Marketing materials to help the brand resonate with both barbershops and customers.

### **4. Value Propositions**

- **For Barbershops**:
    - Simplified booking and **appointment management.**
    - More visibility through online presence and customer reviews.
    - Streamlined payment systems and transaction management.
- **For Customers**:
    - Easy online booking, avoiding long wait times.
    - Access to customer reviews and barbers’ profiles.
    - Convenience of booking from mobile devices.

### **5. Customer Relationships**

- **Self-service**: Customers and barbershops can manage their profiles and bookings independently.
- **Personalized Support**: Offering 24/7 customer service via chat and email for issues.
- **Automated Notifications**: Reminders for upcoming appointments and promotions.

### **6. Channels**

- **Website/App**: The main platform for interacting with the service.
- **Social Media**: Instagram, Facebook, and TikTok for engagement.
- **Email Marketing**: Promotional emails and updates.
- **Word-of-mouth & Referrals**: Encourage users to recommend the platform.

### **7. Customer Segments**

- **Barbershop Owners**: Who need better management tools.
- **Barbers**: Who want exposure and to manage their personal schedules and services.
- **End Customers**: Individuals who want a convenient and quick way to book grooming appointments.

### **8. Cost Structure**

- **Development Costs**: Initial and ongoing costs for building and maintaining the platform.
- **Marketing Costs**: Ads, promotions, influencer campaigns.
- **Salaries**: For your tech, support, and sales teams.
- **Transaction Fees**: Fees for payment processing.
- **Operational Costs**: Hosting, customer support tools, etc.

### **9. Revenue Streams**

- **Subscription Fees**: Monthly or annual fees for barbershops.
- **Booking Transaction Fees**: A percentage of each booking made through the platform.
- **Premium Services**: Add-on services for barbershops (advertising, premium listing, CRM tools).
- **Advertising Revenue**: Partnering with brands to advertise within the platform.
