# Features

# Barber of the Future — AI & AR Business System

## 1. Core Concept

Transform a traditional barber shop from a simple “haircut” service into a premium, tech-driven customer experience that increases loyalty and average revenue per client.

## 2. AI-Powered Features (optional)

- **Face & Hair Scan**: Use AI to analyze face shape, hair density, and texture on arrival. Recommends 5 tailored hairstyles that suit the client’s features.
- **Style Prediction**: AI suggests styles based on trends, face type, and past visits. Stores client history for consistency.
- **Smart Scheduling**: AI predicts when the client’s haircut will grow out and sends automated WhatsApp reminders with available time slots. One-click rebooking.

## 3. Augmented Reality (AR) Preview (optional)

- **Virtual Try-On**: Before cutting, show clients a real-time AR preview of different hairstyles using a smartphone or tablet, similar to Snapchat filters.
- **Confidence Boost**: Clients approve the look before the service starts, reducing dissatisfaction and increasing upsell opportunities.

## 4. Experience-Based Upselling

Move beyond selling just the haircut by offering tiered service packages:

- **Basic**: Standard haircut
- **Executive**: Haircut + hot towel + facial treatment
- **VIP/Manager**: Haircut + massage + coffee + premium products
- **Event Package**: Groom or special occasion styling with dedicated service

Each tier has a clear price and focuses on the overall experience and result, not just the cut.

## 5. Customer Relationship Management (CRM)

- **Loyalty Program**: Automated digital loyalty points via WhatsApp or mobile wallet. Reward after 5 visits with a free service.
- **Automated Reminders**: WhatsApp messages with date, time, and direct booking link when it’s time for the next appointment.
- **Client Database**: Track preferences, visit history, and spending to personalize offers.

## 6. Pricing Strategy

- **Value-Based Pricing**: Charge more by delivering a superior experience, not just the service time.
- **Flexible Tiers**: Offer different price points for different budgets to serve the whole community while maintaining premium revenue from high-value clients.
- **Ethical Approach**: Provide quality service to all clients regardless of budget to build reputation and word-of-mouth.

## 7. Tech Stack Suggestions

- **AI Face Scan**: Use APIs like AWS Rekognition, Google Vision AI, or local Moroccan AI startups.
- **AR Preview**: Integrate with ARCore (Android) or ARKit (iOS), or use no-code platforms like Zappar or 8th Wall.
- **CRM & Messaging**: WhatsApp Business API + simple CRM like HubSpot Free or Moroccan SaaS tools.
- **Payments**: Mobile payment integration via CMI, PayZone, or wallet apps.

## 8. Implementation Roadmap

1. Start with WhatsApp reminders + loyalty program — lowest cost, immediate impact.
2. Add AR preview using a tablet at the chair.
3. Integrate AI face scan as a premium add-on service.
4. Train staff to sell experience packages, not just haircuts.

## 9. Key Success Metrics

- Average ticket value increase
- Client retention rate after 30/60/90 days
- WhatsApp booking conversion rate
- Number of upsells per day
